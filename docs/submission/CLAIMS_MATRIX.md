## Publication constraints

- Use `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3e1KMz23B0` as the Devpost video URL; the channel URL is not a video deliverable.
- Do not describe `judgeRunbook`; it is accepted only as a legacy migration alias and is normalized to `verificationRunbook`.
- Do not describe local persistence or capsule verification as future work; both are implemented.
- The fresh authenticated GPT-5.6 generation, contract/integrity checks, hosted import, ECDSA signing, download, and signed re-import all pass.
- Never say that cryptography proves supplied evidence is true. ECDSA proves key control and unchanged bytes; identity requires independent fingerprint comparison; semantic claims require the cited implementation and tests.
